# PROJECT CONTEXT #
For our final project, we plan to develop a Pac-Man-style pathfinding game inspired by Wumpus World. The game will take place on a 5×5 grid with randomly generated walls. Pac-Man's objective will be to visit all four corners of the map while avoiding a single ghost. Completing the objective will provide a positive reward, while being caught by the ghost will result in a negative reward and end the game.
Pac-Man and the ghost will begin at fixed positions opposite each other. Pac-Man will always know the ghost's location, while the ghost will only detect Pac-Man if they share a row or column without a wall obstructing its line of sight.
We plan to use reinforcement learning to train Pac-Man to navigate the environment while avoiding the ghost. The ghost will use pathfinding when Pac-Man is visible and explore the map when Pac-Man is out of sight. We will compare Pac-Man's performance against simpler baseline strategies and evaluate its win rate across multiple randomly generated maps.
Our target is a Pac-Man win rate between 50% and 90%, which we will use as an indicator of balanced game difficulty. We also want to measure whether the trained agent performs better than a random or basic pathfinding agent.
